Methodology & sources

Observed figures are read directly from a source on a stated date (census, rents, booking states, planning decisions, enrolments). Derived figures are computed from observed ones by a stated rule (earliest delivery year, demand pool, pipeline status and weights, net effective rent, coverage of rental demand). Forecast figures project forward under stated assumptions (provision-rate scenarios, capitalisation sensitivity). Column headers and section titles carry the tag wherever a figure is not observed. Pipeline rows are classified from the register itself (application type, parent references, decision dates, condition discharges) plus dated operator statements; a consent is "completed" only when a live scheme stands on its site.

Scheme census assembled from operator directories, listings platforms, planning records and licensed market research, verified against multiple sources per scheme. Pipeline derived from local-authority planning data with bed counts parsed from application text. Ownership resolved through Companies House PSC chains and Land Registry corporate ownership data.
