Methodology & sources

Observed figures are read directly from a source on a stated date (census, rents, booking states, planning decisions, enrolments). Derived figures are computed from observed ones by a stated rule (expected delivery year, demand pool, overdue status, coverage of rental demand). Forecast figures project forward under stated assumptions (provision-rate scenarios, valuation sensitivity). Column headers and section titles carry the tag wherever a figure is not observed.
